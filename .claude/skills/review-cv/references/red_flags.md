# Red flags

What a recruiter screening for one job reads as a reason to reject. Check every category against the PDF
text, the job (`job_info.md`) and the profile.

## Trajectory
- **Short tenures** (under ~1 year) with no reason on the CV. A short note in the role's meta line, such as
  "(ended in company restructuring)", removes the doubt when the profile has the reason.
- **Unexplained gaps** between roles. Usually interview prep, not CV text.
- **Titles that read as stagnation or demotion:** many years under one plain title with no visible promotion;
  a senior title followed by roles without seniority. The fix is the real title or the promotion, from the profile.
- **Hybrid titles** that suggest leaving engineering for a hands-on engineering job.

## Credibility
- **Implausible metrics:** a number too large for the company's size (compare with its public revenue, users
  or headcount), or one missing its timeframe or basis ("daily", "peak", "per year").
- **Inflating wording:** a plural where it happened once ("peaks"), "led" where the profile says "helped".
- **Counts that don't add up** across the CV: years of a language vs the dates of the jobs that used it.

## Job coverage
- Map every must-have and every listed responsibility in the job to the CV line that proves it.
- A must-have the candidate has but the CV doesn't show is a **wording** fix (or a **question** when the
  profile is silent). One the candidate lacks is a **gap**.
- Practices the job names (code review, CI/CD, hiring, documentation) count only when an experience bullet
  shows them; a skills-list entry alone doesn't prove them.

## Evidence
- The roles that matter most for this job each carry at least one concrete fact: a number, a volume, a named
  system or data type.
- **Vague nouns and adjectives** with nothing behind them: "critical data", "high-performance", "complex",
  "robust". Replace them with the concrete thing from the profile, or cut them.
- **Buzzword bullets:** a bullet a CTO can't picture as work ("championed ... guardrails ... without
  over-engineering"). Rewrite as what was actually done, or cut.

## Voice
- Summary longer than 5 sentences, or in third person ("his", "he").
- Self-praise adjectives (proactive, passionate, clear communicator); the evidence should show them.
- Sentences copied from the job posting. Mirroring its keywords is good for ATS; repeating its phrases reads
  as copy-paste.

## Format and ATS
- More than 2 pages, or a meta line that wraps onto a second line.
- Special characters in job titles (arrows, symbols) that ATS parsers may garble.
- Hedges in the skills list that announce a weakness.

## Accepted costs
These come from tailor-cv's "Write the CV" rules or the profile's "Tailoring rules": mention them at most
as accepted, never as flags. Examples: the bold label on every bullet, the standard section names, the
contact line format, the hedges the profile requires (such as "developer usage").
